Do I need to believe in spirituality to ask questions?

No particular belief is required to ask questions or learn about an offering. You can read about the process, consider the information and decide whether you want to explore it.

How do I know which service is suitable for me?

Start with the service descriptions and their individual FAQs. If you are still unsure, contact Paraṁ Tatva before booking.

Are these services a replacement for professional healthcare or other expert advice?

No. Complementary and spiritual practices should not be presented as substitutes for appropriate medical, mental-health, veterinary, legal or financial advice. The relevant service page should explain its specific scope and limitations.

Have a question?

Start with a conversation.

Ask about a service, its practical details, current fees or upcoming workshops before deciding what to do next.
