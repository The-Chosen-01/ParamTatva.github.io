Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve
clear answers.

General answers about services, pricing, booking and workshops. Each service and workshop also has its own specific FAQs.

Do I need to be familiar with spirituality?

No prior familiarity is needed to read the service information or ask questions. Each service page explains the offering in plain language.

What if I am sceptical?

You can ask practical questions and make your own decision. Spiritual or intuitive interpretations should not be treated as independently verified facts.

Where will I find the current price?

The approved current price and package details will be shown on the relevant service or workshop page before launch. During this draft phase, please enquire for current fees.

How are appointments booked?

Individual sessions will use Cal.com once the relevant event types and booking link have been configured.

How are workshops registered?

Each workshop has its own page. Confirmed dates, seat capacity, fee and registration instructions will be published together.

Do the services replace medical or psychological care?

No. Spiritual and complementary services are not substitutes for appropriate healthcare, psychological, legal, financial, veterinary or other qualified professional advice.

How are testimonials used?

Only genuine feedback approved for publication will be shown. Reviews will not be invented and guaranteed results will not be implied.

Can I contact you before booking?

Yes. The contact page includes a WhatsApp link and a form that prepares a message for you to review and send.
